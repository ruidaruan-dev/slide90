# Capability loop

Use for organization design, talent ladders, operating models, production chains, or cross-functional delivery loops.

## Structure

1. Governing title.
2. Optional gap statement.
3. Three- or four-cell structure ribbon.
4. Two background zones.
5. Five or six connected roles.
6. One feedback line.
7. Target-state conclusion strip.

## Minimum complete chain

```text
Business owner → Domain curator → Data/knowledge → Solution architecture → Delivery → Operations/evaluation
```

Change role names to fit the organization while preserving these functions:

- identify the problem and confirm value;
- maintain authoritative information and permissions;
- prepare data and knowledge assets;
- design a safe, feasible solution;
- build and integrate;
- monitor quality, adoption, and value.

## Card anatomy

Each card contains one role type, one title, and two or three action fragments. Avoid abstract labels such as `ecosystem` unless the actual handoff or feedback mechanism is shown.
