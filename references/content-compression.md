# Content compression

Rewrite vague prose as `action + object + evidence/result`.

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| Continue driving efficiency | Delivery cycle fell by 2 days; 320 hours saved monthly |
| Project progress is good | 5 delivered, 2 on track, 1 awaiting decision |
| Strengthen collaboration | Assign business, delivery, acceptance, and operating owners |
| Improve governance | Add monthly reviews, stage gates, and resource decisions |
| Build a strong team | Develop one architect and add data and evaluation capability |

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer evidence in this order:

1. Business value: revenue, risk, efficiency, quality.
2. Measured result: adoption, cycle time, accuracy, hours saved.
3. Delivery proof: launched project, accepted solution, deployed capability.
4. Operating mechanism: owner, cadence, stage gate, standard.
5. Activity count: meetings, interviews, training sessions.

## Title patterns

- `All four required capabilities have been proven through operating evidence`
- `Close three capability gaps and establish an end-to-end delivery loop`
- `Five metrics are on track, but adoption remains the next constraint`
- `Q3 validates the model; Q4 scales delivery and institutionalizes governance`
- `Choose Option A: it preserves speed while containing implementation risk`

## Card editing

- Remove introductory clauses.
- Use one idea per line.
- Move numbers into a metric ribbon or proof band.
- Remove nouns already visible in the card title.
- Delete text that does not change the management decision.
