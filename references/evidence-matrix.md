# Evidence matrix

Use for role fit, capability proof, internal performance evidence, or any `claim → evidence → judgment` page.

## Structure

1. Governing title.
2. Optional proof-logic subtitle.
3. Four- or five-cell metric ribbon.
4. Two-by-two evidence grid.
5. Conclusion strip.

## Card anatomy

```text
01  Claim or capability
CONTEXT / REQUIREMENT  What must be true
DIRECT EVIDENCE        What happened and what changed
PROOF BAND             Scope | volume | value
```

## Limits

- Claim title: 8 words or fewer.
- Context: 22 words or fewer.
- Evidence: 35 words or fewer.
- Proof band: three facts maximum.
- Keep all four cards identical in size.
- Make evidence visually stronger than context.
